AvitoVIP Transfer Review (2026)

Reviewed and fact-checked 2026-10-09 against official documentation.

AvitoVIP Transfer is a Turkey airport transfer service: you book online before the trip, the price is fixed at checkout (no taxi-meter surprises), and a driver meets you in the arrivals hall with a name sign.

TL;DR: AvitoVIP Transfer sells certainty: the price you see at booking is the price you pay, the driver waits in the arrivals hall with your name sign, and you pay a service built for Turkey's two busiest tourist airports. No meter, no haggling, no surge.

What AvitoVIP Transfer is

AvitoVIP is a Turkey-focused airport transfer service covering Istanbul (IST, SAW) and Antalya (AYT) airports plus city and resort routes — Sultanahmet, Taksim, Belek, Side, Alanya, Kemer and more. You book online before the trip: route, date, flight number, vehicle class. The price is fixed at checkout.

Pricing model: fixed, confirmed at booking

Unlike street taxis (meter + traffic + toll arguments) and unlike surge-priced apps, AvitoVIP confirms the full price when you book. Tolls and parking are included; the driver tracks your flight, so reasonable landing delays don't add waiting fees. Child seats are bookable in advance — something street taxis rarely have.

How pickup works

After booking you get a voucher with meeting instructions. At IST/SAW/AYT the driver waits in the arrivals hall with a name sign, helps with luggage and drives you directly to the hotel address. For the return trip you set the pickup time with a buffer matched to the airport and distance.

Vehicle classes

Standard sedan (up to 3 passengers), comfort/business class, and minivans for families and groups up to 7-8 with luggage. Vehicle class is chosen at booking — the price scales with the class, not with the driver's mood.

Honest limitations

Pre-booked means pre-booked: this is not an on-demand ride-hailing app for trips inside the city. Coverage is Istanbul and Antalya regions — outside Turkey's tourist airports you'll need local operators. And like any fixed-price service, short hops can cost slightly more than a lucky off-peak taxi — you're buying predictability, not the absolute floor price.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an AvitoVIP transfer cost?

The price depends on route and vehicle class and is fixed at booking — what you see at checkout is the final price, tolls included.

Where does the driver meet me?

In the airport arrivals hall, holding a sign with your name. The voucher you receive after booking includes exact meeting-point instructions.

What if my flight is delayed?

The driver tracks your flight number, so reasonable delays are covered without extra waiting fees.

Can I get a child seat?

Yes — child seats are bookable in advance during checkout.
