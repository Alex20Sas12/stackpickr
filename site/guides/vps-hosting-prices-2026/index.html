How Much Does VPS Hosting Cost in 2026? Real Price Tiers

Updated 2026-10-09.

VPS pricing spans from $2.50/mo to $500+/mo, and the sticker price hides the real total. Here's what each tier actually costs — verified September 2026 against official pricing pages.

Tier 1: $2.50–6/mo — raw budget VPS

Vultr ($2.50 IPv6-only, $3.50 with IPv4), Hetzner (€3.29), DigitalOcean ($4–6), Contabo ($5.25), is*hosting ($5.94). You get a raw Linux box: you patch, back up and monitor it yourself. Real total: add 20% for paid backups/snapshots at most providers (free weekly at is*hosting).

Tier 2: $11–25/mo — managed cloud hosting

Cloudways from $11/mo (DigitalOcean 1GB) or $0.016/hr: same raw servers as Tier 1 underneath, but with managed security patches, automated backups, one-click staging, scaling and 24/7 support. The ~$5–7/mo delta over raw VPS is the sysadmin layer.

Tier 3: $25–50/mo — business workloads

2–4GB managed plans for WooCommerce or ~50–100k visits/mo content sites. This is where Kinsta ($35+/mo) and WP Engine ($20–30/mo) compete — WordPress-only, with premium support SLAs.

Hidden costs that break the comparison

  • Backups: DigitalOcean/Hetzner +20% of instance price; included at Cloudways and is*hosting.
  • Egress: AWS/Google Cloud bandwidth is metered — a viral week can cost more than the server. DigitalOcean/Vultr/Hetzner include generous pools.
  • Renewals: Hostinger-style promos can double at renewal; always price the 24-month term.
  • Overselling: Contabo's $5.25 4-vCPU box can underdeliver in practice — cheap CPU is the most commonly oversold resource.

The decision rule

If your hourly rate is above ~$15, self-managing a $4 VPS to save $7/mo is a bad trade — one incident costs more. Below that, raw VPS + your time is legitimate. Either way, price the real total: instance + backups + egress + renewal.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I pay for a VPS in 2026?

$3.29–6/mo for a self-managed box (Hetzner, DigitalOcean, is*hosting); $11–14/mo for managed cloud (Cloudways entry); $25–50/mo for business workloads with support SLAs.

Is a $4 VPS enough for a WordPress site?

Yes, for up to ~10k visits/mo with caching — but you manage updates and backups yourself. Managed entry ($11) removes that work.

What's the cheapest VPS with free backups?

is*hosting from $5.94/mo includes free weekly backups. At DigitalOcean/Hetzner backups cost +20% of the instance price.
