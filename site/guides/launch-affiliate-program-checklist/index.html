Launch an affiliate program: checklist

Updated 2026-10-14.

An affiliate program is a sales channel, not a plugin. Follow this order to launch one that actually recruits partners.

1. Design commissions competitors can beat

Check your niche's standard (10-30% for SaaS/e-commerce). Affiliates compare programs; a weak rate guarantees silence. Recurring commissions on subscriptions dramatically increase affiliate interest.

2. Pick tracking that fits your checkout

Stripe/Paddle subscription? Payment-gateway trackers work. Store (Shopify/WooCommerce/Magento) or custom checkout? Use platform-native tracking — Tapfiliate covers 30+ integrations either way.

3. Set attribution rules

Cookie duration (30-90 days typical), first-touch vs last-touch, coupon attribution. Decide before recruiting so disputes have answers.

4. Build the affiliate assets

Banners, product images, email swipes, honest talking points. Tapfiliate's asset library serves these inside the affiliate portal.

5. Write the terms

Banned traffic (brand bidding, coupon spam), payout thresholds, refund handling. Short and specific beats long and vague.

6. Recruit where affiliates already are

Your existing customers and newsletter first — they convert best. Then affiliate directories and content creators in your niche.

7. Pay on time, automatically

PayPal mass payouts (Tapfiliate Pro) or your preferred rail. Late payments kill programs faster than low commissions.

8. Report monthly

Top affiliates, conversions, EPC. Share wins publicly in your affiliate newsletter.

9. Iterate quarterly

Raise rates on top sellers, prune fraud, refresh creatives. Programs compound — most revenue arrives after month six.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does setup take?

With Tapfiliate: same-day for tracking; a launchable program with assets and terms typically takes a week.

What commission should I offer?

Benchmark your niche: 10-30% one-time for e-commerce, 20-30% recurring common in SaaS.

Do I need a big audience first?

No — start with customers and newsletter subscribers; affiliates join programs that show real conversions.
