How to book an airport transfer in Turkey (step by step)

Updated 2026-10-16.

A pre-booked transfer removes the three classic Turkey airport problems: taxi meters in traffic, price haggling, and finding a car that fits your group and luggage. Here's the exact booking flow, using AvitoVIP Transfer as the worked example.

Step 1 — Choose route and date

Enter the airport (Istanbul IST, Sabiha Gökçen SAW, or Antalya AYT) and your hotel or address. For return trips, book the hotel→airport leg separately with a pickup time that leaves a traffic buffer — Istanbul traffic to IST can add 30-60 minutes at peak.

Step 2 — Pick the vehicle class

Standard sedan fits up to 3 passengers with normal luggage. Families and groups of 4-8 with suitcases should take a minivan — the price difference is small compared to booking two taxis. Business class exists if you want it.

Step 3 — Add your flight number

The flight number lets the driver track your actual landing time. If the flight is delayed, pickup shifts automatically — no waiting-fee argument at 2 a.m.

Step 4 — Add extras (child seats)

Child seats are bookable in advance. Turkish law requires appropriate restraints for children, and street taxis almost never carry them — this alone justifies pre-booking for families.

Step 5 — Confirm and pay

The price shown at checkout is final: tolls, parking and reasonable waiting included. You receive a voucher with the meeting-point instructions and the operator's contact.

Step 6 — Meet the driver at arrivals

After passport control and luggage, walk into the arrivals hall — the driver waits there with a name sign. Show the voucher, load luggage, go. For the return leg, be in the hotel lobby at the agreed pickup time.

Fixed price vs taxi meter: the honest comparison

A metered taxi can be cheaper on a lucky off-peak run — and significantly more expensive in traffic, at night, or with a driver taking the scenic route. The pre-booked fixed price buys predictability: you know the number before you fly. For first-time visitors, families and late-night arrivals, predictability usually wins.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book a Turkey transfer?

At least 24 hours before the flight is comfortable; in high season (June-September) book 2-3 days ahead for minivans.

Is a pre-booked transfer cheaper than a taxi?

Not always the absolute cheapest — but the price is fixed and known in advance, tolls included, with no meter risk in traffic. For families and late arrivals the predictability is worth the difference.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

The driver tracks the flight number and adjusts pickup — reasonable delays don't add fees.

Do I need to pay in advance?

Booking is confirmed online at checkout; the fixed price is locked at that moment.
