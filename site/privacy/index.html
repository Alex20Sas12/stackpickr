Privacy Policy

Updated 2026-10-14.

StackPickr is a static content site. We do not run user accounts, contact forms or newsletters, and we do not set first-party tracking cookies.

What we collect

  • Server logs. Our hosting provider (Vercel) processes standard request logs (IP address, user agent, pages requested) for delivery and security.
  • Advertising. We show ads served by Adsterra. The ad network may set its own cookies and process device data according to its own privacy policy.
  • Affiliate links. Some outbound links are affiliate links (marked by our disclosure). When you click them, the merchant's site may record that you arrived from StackPickr.

What we don't do

  • No cookies, no personal identifiers, no device fingerprinting
  • No sale of personal data
  • No email collection

Analytics

This site uses GoatCounter for aggregate page-view counts only. It sets no cookies, does not identify individual visitors and does not track across sites. The privacy page itself is not counted.

Contact

For privacy questions, reach us via the contact route listed on the About page.
