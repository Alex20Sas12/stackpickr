The pricing math

A 10-person team pays Boardmix Starter $600/year vs Miro Starter $960/year — and Miro Business ($16) jumps to $1,920/year. If AI diagramming matters, the gap widens: Boardmix AI Starter ($10) includes 5,000 credits per seat monthly; Miro Starter gives 25 credits per member per month — a 200× difference in generation allowance.

Where Miro still wins

Ecosystem. Miro's template gallery, community-made frameworks and integration marketplace (Jira, Asana, Slack, Figma, 160+ apps) are deeper. If your workflow lives in those integrations, Miro's premium can be justified.

Where Boardmix wins

Everything price-adjacent: entry cost, AI allowance, and the $99 one-time Lifetime plan — unique among major whiteboards. For solo creators and small teams, the math is not close.

Our verdict

Our verdict: Boardmix for budget-conscious teams and solo creators — $5 entry, 5,000 AI credits, Lifetime option. Miro if your organization already standardizes on its ecosystem.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Boardmix cheaper than Miro?

Yes: $5 vs $8 per member per month at entry (annual billing), and Boardmix has a $99 Lifetime plan Miro doesn't offer.

Does Boardmix have AI features?

Yes — AI diagram, image and text generation. AI Starter includes 5,000 credits per seat per month vs Miro Starter's 25 credits.

Which has a better free tier?

Both allow 3 editable boards. Miro's free plan has a broader template set; Boardmix's is fine for light work.
