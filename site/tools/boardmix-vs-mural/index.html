Facilitation depth

Mural built its name on workshop facilitation: summoning participants, timed activities, voting sessions and private mode are the most refined in the category. Boardmix covers timer/voting/presentation basics — sufficient for most internal meetings, thinner for paid-facilitator workflows.

The pricing math

10-person team: Boardmix Starter $600/year vs Mural Team+ $1,199/year. Add Boardmix's AI credits (5,000/seat/mo at $10) and the value gap grows for teams that generate diagrams or workshop assets with AI.

Our verdict

Our verdict: Boardmix for general team whiteboarding on a budget. Mural for professional facilitators whose clients pay for workshop polish.
