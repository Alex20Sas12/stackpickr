How to read the result

  • Real monthly cost = entry price + backups (+20% at Vultr/Hetzner/DigitalOcean/Contabo/Linode; included at is*hosting and Cloudways).
  • Break-even = how many hours/month of server management make a raw VPS more expensive than $11/mo managed Cloudways, at your hourly rate.
  • Promo prices (Hostinger-style) are first-term only — always check the 24-month renewal price.
