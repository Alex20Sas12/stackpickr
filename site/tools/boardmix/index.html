Boardmix Review (2026)

Reviewed and fact-checked 2026-10-14 against official documentation.

Boardmix is a collaborative online whiteboard with built-in AI: mind maps, flowcharts, presentations and docs on one shared canvas. It undercuts Miro and Mural at entry level and offers a one-time Lifetime plan that neither rival has.

TL;DR: Boardmix is the budget collaborative whiteboard: free tier for 3 boards, Starter $5/member/mo annual ($12 monthly), AI plans from $10, and a $99 one-time Lifetime plan no big rival offers. Undercuts Miro ($8+) and Mural ($9.99+) at entry.

What Boardmix does

Boardmix is a shared-canvas whiteboard for teams: mind maps, flowcharts, diagrams, presentations, sticky notes and documents in one workspace, plus AI generation (diagrams, images, text) on paid AI tiers. Browser-based, real-time collaboration, guest editing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Boardmix free?

Yes — the Free plan includes 3 editable boards with 100 objects each and 1 GB storage, no card required.

How much does Boardmix cost?

Starter is $5/member/mo billed annually ($12 monthly). AI tiers: $10 and $20 annual. A one-time Lifetime plan for 1 member is $99 with 10,000 total AI credits.

Is Boardmix better than Miro?

For price-sensitive teams, yes: cheaper entry, far more AI credits, and a Lifetime option. Miro still has the bigger template and integration ecosystem.

Does Boardmix have AI?

Yes — AI diagramming, image and text generation. AI Starter includes 5,000 credits per seat per month; AI Business 10,000.
