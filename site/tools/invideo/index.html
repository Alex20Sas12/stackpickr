InVideo Review (2026)

Reviewed and fact-checked 2026-10-09 against official documentation.

InVideo is a browser video maker in two flavors: InVideo Studio (template-based editor for marketing and social) and InVideo AI (prompt-to-video with 200+ generative models, including Veo and Kling). Free tier exists; paid unlocks watermark-free export and premium stock.

TL;DR: InVideo covers two jobs: template-based marketing videos (Studio: Free/$15/$30) and prompt-to-video AI (Plus $17/mo with Veo 3.1, Kling 3.0, Seedance 2.5). Cheapest credible way into AI video generation; credits are the real cost driver.

What InVideo does

Two products under one brand: InVideo Studio — browser editor with 6,000+ templates, stock media and text-to-speech for marketing/social videos; InVideo AI — type a prompt, get a video: scripts, scenes, voiceover, using 200+ models including Google Veo 3.1, Kling 3.0 and Seedance 2.5.

The credit math that decides your bill

InVideo's own example: 750 credits ≈ 300 Nano Banana Pro image generations. Video generation models cost more per clip — price your actual workflow before choosing a tier. Plus ($17) is the right test tier: same model shelf as Elite, 53× cheaper. Scale only when credits run out, not before.

Who it's for

Marketers making regular social/promo videos without an editor (Studio), and creators testing AI video generation cheaply (AI Plus). Agencies producing daily will outgrow Plus credits and land on Max/Generative.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is InVideo free?

InVideo Studio has a free plan: templates, 3M+ standard media, AI script generator — with watermarked exports.

How much does InVideo AI cost?

Plus $17/mo (750 credits), Max $85/mo (3,900), Generative $170/mo (8,000), Elite $900/mo (42,500), annual billing. Monthly is ~15% more.

What are InVideo credits?

Credits are InVideo AI's currency for generations. 750 credits ≈ 300 Nano Banana Pro images (InVideo's own example). Video models cost more per clip. Unused credits expire monthly.

Which InVideo is better — Studio or AI?

Studio for template-driven marketing videos you control shot-by-shot; AI for prompt-to-video. Both live in one account; start free on Studio, test AI on Plus.
