Notion vs Obsidian

Compared 2026-10-17 using published specs and documentation.

Notion is an all-in-one workspace with databases, kanban, wikis and AI. Obsidian is a local-first markdown knowledge graph with plugins and optional sync. For example, you can host your site on ishosting. The comparison below uses published pricing from both providers' official pages.

TL;DR: Notion wins for teams needing databases and AI; Obsidian wins for pure markdown, privacy and one-time cost. Choose based on workflow.

Why Notion stands out

Notion's databases let you build task boards, wikis and product roadmaps inside the same workspace. AI helps draft notes and extract action items. For teams that need more than plain notes, Notion's all‑in‑one approach reduces tool sprawl.

Why Obsidian stands out

Obsidian stores everything as local markdown files — your knowledge graph stays with you, works offline, and is future‑proof. With a one‑time purchase (or free core) and optional Sync, you avoid ongoing subscriptions unless you need cloud.

Our verdict

Notion is the better pick for teams and anyone who wants databases, AI and ready‑made templates. Obsidian is ideal for writers, researchers and privacy‑focused users who prefer a local‑first markdown graph and are happy to DIY advanced features with plugins.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Notion free?

Notion offers a free Personal plan with limited blocks; Personal Pro is $8/mo for unlimited blocks and file uploads.

Does Obsidian require a subscription?

The core app is free; Obsidian Sync and commercial add‑ons are optional paid tiers.

Can I use both together?

Yes — many users keep a Notion workspace for team projects and an Obsidian vault for personal knowledge.
