Tapfiliate Review (2026)

Reviewed and fact-checked 2026-10-08 against official documentation.

Tapfiliate is white-label affiliate and referral tracking software. It connects to your checkout or billing system, then tracks referrals, manages affiliates, handles commissions and coupons, and pays out. It's aimed at SaaS and e-commerce teams that want a full affiliate program without enterprise cost.

TL;DR: Tapfiliate is the most complete SMB affiliate platform we've tested: 30+ integrations, white-label portals, multi-tier commissions and built-in PayPal payouts from $89/mo. 14-day free trial.

What Tapfiliate does

Tapfiliate tracks affiliates and referrals for SaaS and e-commerce: connect it to your checkout (native plugin or JS/API), invite affiliates, give them links/coupons, track conversions, manage commissions and pay out. 69,500+ customers per the official site.

Feature walkthrough

  • Tracking: links, coupons, sub-IDs; JS snippet + REST API + webhooks
  • Commission models: flat, percentage, recurring, multi-tier (MLM)
  • Integrations: Shopify, WooCommerce, Stripe, Magento, BigCommerce, Squarespace, Wix + 20 more
  • Affiliate portal with banners/assets (white-label on Pro)
  • Payouts: built-in PayPal mass payout (Pro), manual otherwise
  • Recurring subscription tracking for SaaS
  • Fraud basics: cookie durations, attribution rules

Documentation & support

Clean docs (help.tapfiliate.com), setup guides per integration, and support marketed as 'award-winning human support' — reviewers generally confirm fast, human answers.

Honest limitations

No free tier (trial only). PayPal payouts require Pro. Reporting is solid but not BI-deep; heavy analytics teams export to their own stack. Enterprise partner-network features (recruitment marketplace, reseller channels) belong to PartnerStack-class tools.
