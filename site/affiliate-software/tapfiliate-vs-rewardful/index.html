Compared 2026-10-17 using published specs and documentation.

Why breadth matters

Rewardful's model is elegant — until your stack isn't Stripe. Change your billing provider and the whole affiliate layer breaks. Tapfiliate tracks via JS/API alongside whatever checkout you use.

Feature depth per dollar

White-label portals, multi-tier commissions and PayPal mass payouts arrive on Tapfiliate Pro ($149/mo) — capabilities Rewardful doesn't offer at any price.

Our verdict

Winner: Tapfiliate — more integrations, more commission models, white-label and multi-tier. Rewardful stays a nice niche tool for Stripe-only SaaS.
