Pick Cloudways if…

You run WordPress, WooCommerce or a business app and never want to SSH into a server. Cloudways handles patching, backups, staging, SSL and scaling; its support fixes hosting problems for you.

Pick is*hosting if…

You're technical and want control: a specific country's IP, Windows, Monero/USDT payments, or a box for trading terminals, VPNs and bots. At $5.94 with free backups, it's one of the most flexible raw VPS offers available.

Our verdict

For our readers — mostly business and site owners — Cloudways is the recommended default: it removes the entire server-operations category of risk. is*hosting is our recommendation for technical users and international/crypto use cases.
