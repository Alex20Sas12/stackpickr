Hourly vs monthly

Vultr's hourly billing is ideal for ephemeral dev boxes. For anything that runs permanently, is*hosting's monthly model with term discounts (~15% annual) is simpler and includes backups Vultr bills extra for.

The location edge

LatAm (Peru, Colombia, Chile, Argentina, Brazil), CIS (Kazakhstan, Ukraine) and Gulf (UAE, Turkey) locations exist only on is*hosting's map.

Our verdict

Winner: is*hosting for permanent international workloads — locations, backups and billing flexibility. Vultr keeps its crown for short-lived dev instances.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Vultr cheaper than is*hosting?

At the absolute entry ($2.50 IPv6-only), yes. Comparable IPv4 configs are close, and is*hosting includes free backups.

Can I get Windows on either?

is*hosting sells Windows VPS natively from $10.19/mo; Vultr charges Windows licensing on top.

Which has better uptime?

is*hosting contracts 99.5%+ network SLA (marketing 99.99%); Vultr guarantees 100% uptime SLA with credits — both solid on paper.
