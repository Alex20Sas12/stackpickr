Cloudways vs Hetzner

Compared 2026-10-14 using published specs and documentation.

TL;DR: Hetzner wins the raw price war (€3.29/mo). Cloudways wins where it matters for businesses: management, global locations, backups, support — and you can never lose an evening to a server outage you're not qualified to fix.

Why Hetzner is beloved

Nothing beats Hetzner's price-to-hardware ratio, and hobby projects or developer side-servers live there happily. It's the standard recommendation for technically skilled users who want Europe-based raw compute.

Why businesses choose Cloudways instead

Hetzner gives you a server and silence. Outages, patches, migrations and tuning are your job. Cloudways' global footprint (65+ locations vs 4 regions) also matters if your audience isn't in central Europe.

Our verdict

Winner: Cloudways for business workloads — management, support and global reach. Hetzner remains the best raw-value pick for developers who manage everything themselves.
