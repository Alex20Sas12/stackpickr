is*hosting vs OVHcloud

Compared 2026-10-08 using published specs and documentation.

OVHcloud is a European giant with aggressive VPS pricing. is*hosting sells self-managed KVM/NVMe VPS in 42 datacenters across 40 countries, with crypto payments and free weekly backups. Specs below are from official pages (September 2026).

TL;DR: OVH wins in raw EU capacity and anti-DDoS pedigree. is*hosting wins for global reach, crypto payments, Windows VPS and support responsiveness — with a comparable entry price.

OVH's strengths

Massive EU capacity, always-on anti-DDoS included, and cheap starter RAM. If you're Europe-centric and love big providers, OVH is a solid institution.

Where is*hosting overtakes

40-country map vs OVH's handful, crypto billing (BTC/USDT/XMR...), native Windows VPS, free weekly backups and English+Russian 24/7 support with free basic administration. For international or crypto-paying customers, OVH can't compete.

Our verdict

Winner: is*hosting for international projects and crypto payers. OVHcloud remains a strong EU-centric pick for raw capacity.
