Cloudways vs WP Engine

Compared 2026-10-08 using published specs and documentation.

WP Engine is one of the oldest premium managed WordPress hosts. Cloudways adds a full managed layer — 24/7 support, backups, security patching, staging and scaling tools — on top of raw cloud servers. The comparison below uses published pricing from both providers' official pages.

TL;DR: Cloudways undercuts WP Engine on price with no visit caps and multi-app support. WP Engine's strength is its WordPress-only polish and enterprise features at higher tiers.

Price per real capacity

WP Engine's entry plan caps visits and installs. Cloudways' $11 server has no artificial caps, and you scale resources with a click when traffic grows — usually cheaper than jumping WP Engine tiers.

If you only run WordPress

WP Engine's platform is undeniably refined. But its premium shows in price, and you're locked into a WordPress-only ecosystem. Cloudways keeps every future option open.

Our verdict

Winner: Cloudways on value and flexibility. WP Engine remains a premium option for WordPress-only teams that want maximum platform polish.

CloudwaysCDN is available as a cheap add-on; the base platform is already cache-optimized.
