What is*hosting is

WEBRAIN OÜ (Estonia, since 2005) runs KVM VPS/VDS, dedicated servers, storage servers and dedicated VPN. Claims 50,000+ customers and 100,000+ servers. The differentiator is the map: 42 datacenters, 40 countries, 5 continents — including rare locations like Serbia, Kazakhstan, Peru, Colombia, Chile, Turkey and UAE.

Uptime: read both numbers

Marketing pages say 99.99%. The contractual SLA guarantees ≥99.5% network availability. We present both because the gap matters when credits are on the line.

Honest limitations

Self-managed by default (free admin is basic-level). A minority of reviews report KYC friction (passport/card-photo requests), occasional node instability and support escalation gaps on hardware faults. Not an offshore/DMCA-ignored host — the AUP explicitly prohibits infringement, despite privacy-friendly rhetoric.
