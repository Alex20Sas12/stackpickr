2. Hetzner — Best for raw price-to-hardware (EU)

From €3.29/mo. Unbeatable EU specs and clean network. Weaknesses: only 4 regions, no management, paid backups.

3. DigitalOcean — Best for developer ecosystem

From $4-6/mo. Best marketplace/docs in the class. Weaknesses: unmanaged, snapshots cost extra.

4. Vultr — Best for hourly dev instances

From $2.50/mo. 32 datacenters, true hourly billing. Weaknesses: support is ticket-level only.

5. Contabo — Best for RAM per dollar

$5.25/mo for 4 vCPU/6GB. Weaknesses: overselling complaints, support delays in 2025-2026 reviews.

6. Linode (Akamai) — Best for straightforward VPS

From $5/mo. Solid all-rounder, 24 regions. Weaknesses: self-managed, Akamai pivot focused on enterprise.

7. OVHcloud — Best for EU capacity + anti-DDoS

From ~$5.16/mo. Always-on DDoS protection. Weaknesses: limited regions, support reputation mixed.

8. Hostinger VPS — Best for beginner pricing

Frequent promos under $6/mo. Weaknesses: renewal price jumps, fewer locations.
