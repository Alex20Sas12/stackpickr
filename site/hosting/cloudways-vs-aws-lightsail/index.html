Cloudways vs AWS Lightsail

Compared 2026-10-17 using published specs and documentation.

Lightsail is Amazon's simplified VPS product with bundled bandwidth caps. Cloudways adds a full managed layer — 24/7 support, backups, security patching, staging and scaling tools — on top of raw cloud servers. The comparison below uses published pricing from both providers' official pages.

TL;DR: Cloudways wins on predictability and management. Lightsail's $3.50 price balloons once you exceed bundled transfer (from $0.09/GB) or need backups, static IPs beyond quota, and support — while Cloudways includes all of it from $11/mo.

The hidden Lightsail bill

Lightsail pricing looks friendly until real traffic arrives: extra transfer is billed per GB, snapshots cost per GB-month, static IPs get charged when detached, and actual AWS support requires a paid plan. Several reviewers report small bills doubling once a site grows.

Predictability wins

Cloudways gives you a fixed monthly number with bandwidth, backups and engineers included. If you specifically need AWS infrastructure, Cloudways also offers AWS EC2 tiers — managed.

Our verdict

Winner: Cloudways. Lightsail is a decent sandbox for AWS experiments. For production sites, Cloudways' all-inclusive price and managed service beat Lightsail's nickel-and-diming.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is AWS Lightsail really $3.50/month?

Only at minimum usage. Extra bandwidth, snapshots and detached IPs are billed on top; AWS support costs extra.

Can Cloudways host on AWS?

Yes — Cloudways offers managed AWS EC2 tiers alongside DigitalOcean, Vultr, Linode and Google Cloud.

Which is easier for WordPress?

Cloudways — Lightsail WordPress images still require you to manage updates, caching, backups and security.
