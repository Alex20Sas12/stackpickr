Cloudways vs Linode (Akamai)

Compared 2026-10-17 using published specs and documentation.

Linode — now part of Akamai — sells straightforward raw VPS/Cloud Compute instances. Cloudways adds a full managed layer — 24/7 support, backups, security patching, staging and scaling tools — on top of raw cloud servers. The comparison below uses published pricing from both providers' official pages.

TL;DR: Cloudways is the better choice for website owners: Linode gives you a raw server and a ticket queue, Cloudways gives you managed hosting engineers. Linode infrastructure is even selectable inside Cloudways.

Where Linode wins

Raw price and the Akamai enterprise ecosystem. If you're building on Akamai's edge network with your own engineering team, Linode is a natural fit.

Where Cloudways wins

Everything around the server. Deployments, SSL, backups, scaling, staging and 24/7 engineers who actually fix hosting problems. For WordPress/WooCommerce/Magento workloads, the tooling gap is enormous.

Our verdict

Winner: Cloudways for businesses and agencies. Linode stays a solid raw VPS for self-sufficient developers — and Cloudways customers can run on Linode hardware with the managed layer on top.
