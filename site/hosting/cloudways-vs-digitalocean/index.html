Cloudways vs DigitalOcean

Compared 2026-10-16 using published specs and documentation.

DigitalOcean sells raw cloud servers (Droplets). Cloudways adds a full managed layer — 24/7 support, backups, security patching, staging and scaling tools — on top of raw cloud servers. The comparison below uses published pricing from both providers' official pages.

TL;DR: Choose Cloudways if you want a business site running without DevOps work — managed backups, security and 24/7 engineer support from $11/mo. Choose DigitalOcean only if you have a sysadmin and want the cheapest raw resources.

The real cost difference

DigitalOcean's $4 Droplet has no backups, no managed security, no staging and no one to call when PHP breaks. Recreating the Cloudways feature set on DigitalOcean means paying for snapshots, configuring firewalls, installing server management tools — and spending your own hours. For a business site, the $7 difference usually pays for itself in the first incident.

When DigitalOcean still makes sense

Developers who enjoy managing servers, or teams with dedicated DevOps, can save money on DigitalOcean's raw prices. Cloudways runs on DigitalOcean infrastructure too — so you keep DO datacenters and get the managed layer on top.

Our verdict

Winner: Cloudways. For anyone running a business website, agency or store, Cloudways delivers the same DigitalOcean hardware plus everything DigitalOcean leaves out — backups, security, staging, scaling and 24/7 support — for a small premium. It is the safer default pick.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Cloudways more expensive than DigitalOcean?

On paper yes ($11 vs $4 entry). But Cloudways includes managed backups, security patching, staging and 24/7 support that DigitalOcean charges extra for or doesn't offer at all.

Does Cloudways run on DigitalOcean servers?

Yes — DigitalOcean is one of five providers you can choose under the hood, alongside Vultr, Linode, AWS and Google Cloud.

Can I try Cloudways before paying?

Yes, a 3-day free trial with no credit card required.
