Cloudways Review (2026)

Reviewed and fact-checked 2026-10-09 against official documentation.

Cloudways is a managed cloud hosting platform: you pick the raw server underneath (DigitalOcean, Vultr, Akamai Linode, AWS or Google Cloud) and Cloudways handles everything on top — provisioning, security, backups, scaling and support. It targets businesses that want cloud performance without hiring a DevOps engineer.

TL;DR: Cloudways is the easiest way to run serious websites on real cloud infrastructure without DevOps. From $11/mo: managed DO/Vultr/Linode/AWS/GCP servers with backups, staging and 24/7 engineers. 3-day free trial, no card.

What Cloudways actually is

Cloudways is a management platform, not a datacenter. You choose the underlying provider — DigitalOcean, Vultr, Akamai Linode, AWS or Google Cloud — and Cloudways provisions and runs it: security patching, firewalls, automated backups, staging, SSL, caching and 24/7 support. 65+ datacenter locations across all five providers.

Apps you can launch in one click

WordPress, WooCommerce, Magento, Laravel, Symfony, Joomla, Drupal, PrestaShop and more — each with its optimized stack. The same dashboard manages all of them.

Support and documentation

24/7 chat and ticket support from hosting engineers (not scripted first line), phone support on request, plus an extensive knowledge base at support.cloudways.com.

Two product lines: Flexible vs Autonomous

Cloudways now runs two lines. Flexible is the classic hourly pay-as-you-go managed server described above. Autonomous is a Kubernetes-based WordPress tier with autoscaling and visit-based pricing ($35–$995/mo by traffic band) for teams that want hands-off scaling. Most readers will land on Flexible — it's where the $11/mo entry lives.

Honest limitations

You pay a premium over raw servers ($11 vs $4-5 entry). No native Windows hosting. Email hosting isn't included (use the $1.50 Rackspace add-on or external). The platform is opinionated — unusual stack requests live outside its scope.
