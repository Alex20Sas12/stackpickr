7 Cheapest VPS Providers in 2026 (Real Entry Prices)

Updated 2026-10-09.

'Cheap VPS' is a minefield: promo prices that triple at renewal, oversold nodes, paid backups. All prices below are verified entry prices from official pricing pages (September 2026), not promo screenshots.

1. Vultr — Best for cheapest hourly-billed instance

From $2.50/mo (1 vCPU/512MB, IPv6-only) and $3.50/mo with IPv4. 32 datacenters. True hourly billing — spin up for a day, pay for a day. The catch: support is ticket-only and the $2.50 tier sells out in popular regions.

2. Hetzner — Best for cheapest usable specs (EU/US)

From €3.29/mo (CX22: 2 vCPU/4GB). Nothing in the US or EU touches this price-to-hardware ratio. The catch: only 4 regions, strict signup vetting, unmanaged, backups cost extra.

3. DigitalOcean — Best for cheapest with real docs & ecosystem

From $4/mo (Basic 512MB) and $6/mo (1GB). Best-in-class tutorials and one-click apps. The catch: snapshots and backups are paid add-ons — price the real total.

4. Contabo — Best for most RAM per dollar

$5.25/mo for 4 vCPU/6GB — spec-wise nothing is close. The catch: persistent overselling complaints and support delays through 2025–2026; fine for dev/test, risky for production SLAs.

5. is*hosting — Best for cheap VPS in 40+ countries incl. offshore

From $5.94/mo (1 vCPU/1GB/20GB NVMe), 42 datacenters in 40 countries, crypto payments, DMCA-tolerant zones. The catch: self-managed, and marketing uptime (99.99%) exceeds the contractual SLA (99.5%).

6. Linode (Akamai) — Best for cheap all-rounder

From $5/mo (1GB, Nanode $5). 24 regions, flat pricing, solid network. The catch: self-managed, no managed layer unless you add Cloudways on top.

7. Hostinger VPS — Best for cheapest first-year promos

Frequent promos under $6/mo on 12–24-month terms. The catch: renewal jumps — read the second-year price before committing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cheapest VPS that's actually usable?

Hetzner CX22 at €3.29/mo (2 vCPU/4GB) is the cheapest usable production box in the EU. Below $3 you get IPv6-only or sold-out tiers (Vultr $2.50).

Is a cheap VPS good for WordPress?

Yes up to ~10k visits/mo on 1–2GB RAM with caching. Managed hosting (e.g. Cloudways from $11/mo) adds backups, staging and support on top — cheaper than your time if you're not a sysadmin.

Why do 'cheapest VPS' promo prices differ from renewal prices?

Hostinger-style promos discount the first term only. Always check the renewal price per month on 24-month terms before comparing.

What does 'cheap' really cost with backups?

Add paid backup/snapshot costs: DigitalOcean +20% of droplet price, Hetzner +20%. A $4 VPS with backups is $4.80–5. Free weekly backups are included only at is*hosting.
