Why a transfer instead of a taxi

  • Price known upfront. €50 is the final amount. Airport taxis in Istanbul often end up costing more due to traffic and the meter, and "tourist surcharges" happen in high season.
  • Pay after the ride. No deposit — you pay the driver in cash or by card on arrival.
  • Meet & greet. The driver waits at arrivals with a name sign and helps with luggage. Flight delayed? Waiting is free.
  • Own fleet. Mercedes Vito, V-Class and minivans — the company drives its own cars, not a marketplace of random contractors.
  • Free cancellation up to 24h and 24/7 WhatsApp/Telegram support in Russian and English.

FAQ

How much does the transfer Antalya Airport (AYT) → Belek cost?

From €50 for the whole vehicle (a minibus seats 6 with luggage). Fixed price: no meter, no night/surcharge/waiting fees.

What if my flight is delayed?

The company tracks your flight number: the driver arrives for the actual landing time, waiting is free.

How do I pay?

No prepayment when booking. Pay the driver after the ride: cash (EUR/USD/TRY) or card, incl. MIR via SBP.

Can I get a child seat?

Yes — request it when booking; provided free or for a symbolic fee depending on car class.

What if my plans change?

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before pick-up.

Verdict: for a family or group of 3–6, a €50 transfer is often cheaper than a metered taxi and guaranteed more predictable: car, price and driver are known in advance.
