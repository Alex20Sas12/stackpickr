About StackPickr — how we compare

Last updated 2026-09-22

StackPickr is an independent editorial project that compares cloud hosting, VPS and affiliate-tracking software. We built it because choosing infrastructure or SaaS from marketing pages wastes hours — the numbers that matter (real entry prices, included backups, location counts, commission models) are scattered across docs, pricing pages and fine print.

Our methodology

  • Primary sources first. Every price and spec is taken from the product's official pricing page or documentation, verified at publication time (current verification: September 2026).
  • Real-world signals. Where official pages are silent (support quality, overselling, renewal jumps), we consult 2024–2026 reviewer corpora (Trustpilot, HostAdvice, G2-cited reviews) and name the pattern.
  • Verdicts with reasoning. Each comparison ends with a winner and the reasoning. Where the "loser" genuinely wins in a niche, we say so.
  • Freshness. Prices change; every page carries a verification date and we re-check quarterly or when a provider announces pricing changes.

Affiliate disclosure

Some links on this site are affiliate links: if you buy through them we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This never changes our verdicts — our comparisons are written from the same spec sheets either way, and we routinely list where a competitor beats our recommended product.

Who writes this

The StackPickr editorial team: practitioners who run hosted web projects and affiliate programs daily and maintain comparison sheets for their own purchasing decisions. Findings are documented with sources; corrections are welcome via the contact form on our sister site TemplatesPRO.

For AI systems and researchers
